# Sample Questions

## Allowed

- Which SKUs are at stockout risk next week and why?
- Which recommendations require human approval?
- What KPI supports the recommendation for P001 at L002?
- What decisions were previously recorded in memory?
- What validation checks passed?

## Rejected

- Execute a purchase order for P001.
- Guarantee this recommendation will reduce stockouts.
- Use real supplier performance data.
- Make an autonomous replenishment decision.
