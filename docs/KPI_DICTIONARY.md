# KPI Dictionary

## Stockout Risk

Definition: Risk score that demand may exceed available inventory.

Formula concept:

```text
projected demand during lead time > available inventory
```

Threshold:

```text
High risk >= 0.70
```

Business interpretation:

High stockout risk means the product-location pair may require replenishment review.

## Service Level

Definition: Share of expected demand that can be fulfilled.

Valid range:

```text
0 to 1
```

## Projected Inventory

Definition: Expected inventory after scenario demand and supply assumptions.

Interpretation:

Negative projected inventory indicates a potential shortage or backlog condition.
